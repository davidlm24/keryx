# PASS 3 — Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA + Windows)

**Skill:** `design:accessibility-review` was available and used as the checklist
(its WCAG 2.1 AA reference). Target size is judged against WCAG 2.2 **2.5.8**
(24 × 24 px), as the pass asks.

**Code:** branch `audit/design-0219` @ 0866cb4 (0.2.19 candidate). Audit only:
nothing was edited.

**How it was checked (16/10/2026)**
- Source: `src/` (not `src/components/ui/`), `docs/design-system/tokens.css`
  and `components/bundle.css`, `src/components/console/console.css`,
  `electron/main.cjs`.
- Side-panel preview (`npm run dev`, sample data), dark, at 1366 × 768 and
  1280 × 720 (≈ 1920 × 1080 at 150 %). Keyboard tried with real key presses
  (Tab, Enter, Ctrl+Enter, ↓, Esc); Tab order, accessible names, focus styles
  and target sizes measured with scripts in the page.
- Contrast computed from the tokens (WCAG relative luminance; `color-mix()`
  mixed in sRGB as the browser does). Table at the end.
- Not possible in the preview: forced colours, `prefers-reduced-motion`
  emulation, a real screen reader, Shift+F10 / the Menu key, Windows "Text
  size". Those parts come from the code and are listed under **Needs manual
  check**.

**Summary:** 21 findings: **1 Blocker, 15 Major, 5 Minor.**
What already works well:
- **Names:** the scan found no unnamed control in the console or in
  Configurações › Telas e saídas. Icon buttons take their tooltip as their
  name (`IconBtn`), and switches are `role="switch"` with `aria-checked`.
- **Focus ring:** the gold ring on buttons and rows is clear: 6.2–7.4:1, and
  4.0:1 even on a gold on-air row.
- **Targets:** every target measured meets 2.5.8. Small buttons are 24 px by
  design; the 28 × 16 switches and 20 px segments pass by spacing.
- **Reduced motion:** the console honours it through one blanket rule
  (`bundle.css:17`), plus rules for the drag ghost and the window.
- **Live keys:** they don't fight a focused control. Text fields and selects
  take every key; radio groups, splitters, the seek bar and menus keep their
  arrows; behind the window only B and L act, as specified.
- **Text contrast:** every text token pair passes 4.5:1 at rest. The only
  exceptions are a hover (A11Y-20) and dimmed rows (A11Y-17).
