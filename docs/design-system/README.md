KERYX is the live operator console a Brazilian church volunteer runs for 90 minutes from a dark booth: lyrics, Bible passages, videos and announcements go out to several screens while 300 people watch the result on a 4-meter wall. The system exists so that every screen, module and future feature keeps three things readable at a glance — what is on air, what goes next, whether every output is connected — and makes the wrong action hard and the right one instant. Dark only. Colour means state and nothing else.

`CLAUDE.md` records the owner's decisions and wins wherever this page disagrees; `docs/design-system.md` has the behaviour and the layout. History is at the end of that page, not here.

## Principles

- **Programa / Prévia discipline.** A single click never reaches the screens: it prepares (`prev`). What is on air changes only on a deliberate act: `Enviar ao ar` where there is one, Enter or Ctrl+Enter, a double click on the item, a drag onto the Programa, or ←/→ moving the slide already on air. Typing never sends. Design every new feature so that this stays true; the full rules are in `docs/design-system.md` › Comportamento.
- **Colour is state.** `air` red = on air, `prev` green = prepared, `rh` blue = screens locked (*Travar telas*), `warn` amber = warning or failure. `brand` gold is the KERYX identity — the logo mark, the active environment's underline, the focus ring of controls, ordinary primary actions (Salvar, Novo culto) — and one live mark: **the slide or verse on air** in the Slides column, No ar and the Bíblia's lists is gold. Everything else is one of four warm greys. If a colour is not telling the operator a state, remove it.
- **Dense, small, quiet.** 11px telemetry, 12px UI, 13–14px content, nothing larger except `live-title` (16px) and `clock` / `countdown`. Bare glyphs, no boxes around icons, no inline help. The pills are the monitors' captions inside their screens (the Prévia's badge, the Programa's pill), the owner's choice. The actions that change what the audience sees (Tela preta, Logo, Limpar, Encerrar) are glyphs in the Programa's pill, named by their tooltips and aria-labels. Explanations live in tooltips and empty states; a panel carries no text it does not need.
- **One rhythm.** A 4px grid, 28px rows and controls, 24px controls in panel headers, a 6px gutter between modules (the splitter is the gap), 8px radius on controls, 14px on panels. Hover and state changes take 120ms ease-out; folds and the window's resize ease; a slide change on the screens uses the theme's transition.

## Content fundamentals

Write Brazilian Portuguese, never European forms: *tela*, *salvar*, *arquivo*, *excluir*, *cronômetro*, *celular*, *usuário*. Address the operator as *você* only when unavoidable; prefer the imperative (*Enviar ao ar*, *Pôr na prévia*, *Localizar…*). Sentence case everywhere except section headers, which the `section` style sets uppercase. No exclamation marks on the console, no emoji, no jokes while something is on air.

Use these words exactly and never a synonym: **Programa, Prévia, Próximo, Travar telas, Destravar, Enviar ao ar, Pôr na prévia, Ir para o item, Voltar ao anterior, Tela preta, Mostrar logo, Limpar texto, Encerrar, Ver, Densidade, Automática, Confortável, Compacta, Editar disposição, Setlist, Biblioteca, Letras, Mídia, Avisos, Camadas, Temas, Tema, Fundo, Papel de parede, Saídas, Conectada, Reconectando, Desconectada, Fechada, Reabrir, Esquecer.** One word per concept: *Papel de parede* is what the screens show with nothing on air, *Tema* what goes on air with an item (text and background), *Fundo* the layer's name; *Excluir* deletes, *Tirar* takes out of a place, *Remover* only in *Remover saída*; *Tela* is a physical monitor or a screen on the network, *Saída* a window or page; *Exemplo*, never "amostra". The output of the avisos in sequence is **Avisos**. The rundown is the **Setlist**.

Captions follow one pattern, middle dots as separators, counts as `2/5`: `Prévia · Firme na rocha · 2/5`, `2 telas`, `Nenhuma saída aberta`, `Palco desconectada às 19:42`, `Reconectando… tentativa 2`. The Programa's pill sets its caption in parts: the state's dot, the title (unless the player already names it), the position (`2/6`) and how many screens show it (`2 telas`; amber when fewer than expected). Times are `19:46`, durations `4:30`. Numbers are always tabular (the `sans` family is loaded with `tnum`).

A warning names the thing and the way out in one line: `Arquivo não encontrado · Localizar…`, `Fonte "Bebas Neue" não instalada · usando Geist`. A question says what happens; its buttons are in Windows order, the action then **Cancelar** on the right. A question that cannot be taken back (Excluir, Descartar, any `danger` action) opens on **Cancelar**, so Enter on an untouched dialog and Esc both keep everything; *Encerrar*, which has Desfazer, keeps its action focused.

## Colour

Grounds, from the window out: `bg-0` is the window (and the top bar, which has no panel), `bg-1` every module, `bg-2` fields, popovers and hover, `bg-3` pressed or selected. These four greys and the two hairlines (`line`, `line-strong`) are the whole neutral palette; do not add a fifth step. Two edges derive from them for what floats (Rodada N4): `line-overlay`, the one ring of every window, dialog, menu, popover, tooltip and toast, and `line-soft` (`line` at 60 %), the lines inside them. Text is `fg-1` for content and the row in hand, `fg-2` for UI labels and glyphs at rest, `fg-3` for metadata, headers and placeholders. All three pass 4.5:1 on the surfaces they are allowed on; never set text in `line-strong` or dimmer.

State colours come in four tokens each — the mark (`air`), the text (`air-fg`), the fill (`air-fill`) and the wash (`air-tint`) — and the same for `prev`, `rh` and `warn` (use `on-warn` on an amber fill). Marks are frames, bars and dots (the Programa pill's dot); text tokens are the lighter variants that read on `bg-1`; fills are rare — *Enviar ao ar* and a switch that is on (green), *Destravar* on the lock banner (blue), *Encerrar*, *Excluir* and the "drop to send" hint over the Programa (red) — and text on a fill is `on-state`. A fill button steps **darker** on hover and pressed (`*-fill-hover`, `*-fill-pressed`) so white stays above 4.5:1; disabled goes neutral (`bg-3`, `fg-3`) and its focus ring is `fg-1`, as on the gold button. A list row (`kx-row`: the Setlist, the Biblioteca) in a state takes its state's 10% tint with the 2px bar (`bar`) on its left edge, and the title takes the state's text colour; a tint never appears without its bar. Slide and verse rows are the exception: their state is the background only, no border and no side bar — on air the gold wash (`--live-bg`, 24 % `brand` over `bg-2`), in the Prévia a 24 % `prev` wash.

Red and green are distinguishable by lightness (4.7:1 and 5.8:1 on `bg-1`, text variants 6.7:1 and 9.4:1). Outside the slide and verse lists a state also carries a word (`Prévia`, `Travado`, `Conectada`, `Reconectando`, `Nada no ar`). In the Slides column, No ar and the Bíblia's verses, on air (gold) and Prévia (green) differ by colour only, with no glyph (owner 2026-10-16): the two hues are far apart, and both at once show green with the number on a gold chip.

`brand` gold (`#C19959`) marks identity and ordinary interaction, sparingly — one or two gold cues per region: the KERYX mark, the gold underline of the environment on screen (KERYX | BÍBLIA), the 2px focus ring of controls (5.5:1 on `bg-3`), the slide or verse on air, the bars of a report chart, the drag ghost's icon and drop slot, and the gold button `kx-btn-brand` for non-live primary actions (Salvar, Novo culto, Importar). The gold button's label is `on-brand` (6.7:1); hover is `brand-hover`, pressed `brand-pressed` (4.7:1, never as text on `bg-2`/`bg-3`), disabled goes neutral (`bg-3`, `fg-3`), and its focus ring turns `fg-1` so it shows on the fill. `brand-tint` is a 10% wash for the rare neutral selection that needs a gold edge (an empty Temas slot on hover). Gold never recolours the Programa's pill, the Prévia, *Enviar ao ar*, a locked state, an output fault or a warning, and never appears on a projected screen.

Projected screens are a different world: `screen-bg` black, `screen-fg` white text with the `screen-text` shadow, the church's themes and backgrounds. Console tokens never leak onto a screen and screen styles never appear on the console.

## Typography

One family, `sans` (Geist, shipped with the app, never loaded from the internet, with `tnum`; the system font only if it ever failed to load). Seven sizes, the `fs-*` tokens (Rodada DS2): 11, 12, 13, 14, 16, 20 and 28 px. Twelve console styles, three screen styles:
- **Everyday:** `meta` 11/16 for telemetry (times, counts, key, length: never the name of an item that is on air or prepared); `section` 11/16 500 uppercase with 0.06em tracking for module headers; `ui` 12/16 and `ui-strong` 12/16 500 for controls; `content` 13/18 for titles in lists; `content-lg` 14/20 for slide and verse text.
- **Live information** (it gets its own steps so it can be found at a glance): `live-cap` 12/16 600 for a monitor's state word (the Prévia's badge), in the state's colour; `live-title` 16/22 600 for what is on air where a monitor carries a caption, once per screen; `next-title` 14/20 500 for what is prepared; `alert-title` 13/18 600 for what failed in an output banner.
- **Time:** `clock` 20/24 and `countdown` 28/32.

On the console nothing is larger than 14px except `live-title` (16px) and those two. Weight is 400 or 500 for everyday text; 600 belongs to the live steps and to projected lyrics and the cover.

Screens use `display` (the same family at heavier weight): `screen-lyrics`, `screen-verse`, `screen-ref`, sized in container units of the monitor so a preview at 320px wide and the real 1920px screen keep the same proportions.

## Spacing, radii, layout

`space-1` (4px) is the unit. Rows and controls are `row` (28px) tall; list rows are `row-comfortable` (32px) in the Confortável density. The top bar is `topbar` (40px, on `bg-0` with no panel: the search is a round `bg-1` field, Ver and the bell round `bg-1` chips). The console's gutter is **6px** (`.console` overrides the `gutter` token's 8px): one gutter between every container, and **the splitter is the gap** — never a second gap beside one. Panel headers are 32px (28 in Compacta), one line, text 12px (`space-3`) from the edge, last button 12px from the right edge, controls 24px tall and 8px apart; a band under a header is 36px with 28px controls, 4px apart; anything with a box of its own sits 4px in from its column. Buttons and rows' actions are at least 24 × 24px (WCAG 2.2 target size); the glyph inside stays 16px. Popovers and menus pad `space-3`; dialogs `space-4`. Radii: `radius-control` on anything you click or type into, on list-row cards and banners; `radius-panel` on modules, popovers, dialogs and THE window; `radius-round` on 6px state dots, the seek handle, the Programa's pill and the top bar's search and chips.

The console is a **dock** per environment (culto and Bíblia): splits and tab groups the operator arranges with **Ver › Editar disposição**, saved per computer, with named arrangements. The culto's default follows three width tiers measured on the grid (wide ≥ 1400px: Biblioteca | Slides | the centre — Programa with the player, then Prévia and the Painel — | Camadas, Temas, Setlist, with No ar along the bottom; medium ≥ 1100px: no right column, Camadas a drawer from the Programa's pill; narrow: also Biblioteca over Slides). The Programa, the Prévia and the Setlist never leave; nothing ever scrolls the whole console. `docs/design-system.md` › Layout has the details.

Every module has a header (`section` style, its + and its fold chevron at the right), can be resized with a splitter, folded to its header (a 28px strip across a row), or put in the tray and brought back from **Ver** (the eye beside the bell: the modules, *Densidade*, *Disposição*, *Editar disposição*, *Restaurar disposição*, *Atalhos do teclado*). The Programa, the Prévia, the player and the Bíblia's panels do not fold. A splitter shows a grip on hover and on keyboard focus; Tab reaches it, ←/→ (↑/↓ for a horizontal one) resize in 16px steps, a double click restores. Columns that cannot be resized are separated by a plain `kx-gap`, with no grip.

## States

**List rows are inset cards**: every `kx-row`/`kx-layer` sits 4px in from its column with `radius-control`, 2px from the next, so hover and states never run edge to edge (the Slides column and No ar go edge to edge, the owner's exception). A row in a state is a 2px bar on its left edge in the state colour plus the matching 10% tint. Hover adds `bg-2` and lightens the glyph (`fg-2` → `fg-1`); pressed is `bg-3`; a selected-but-neutral row is `bg-3`. Done items take `dim` opacity on the whole row. Disabled controls take `dim` and lose hover.

**Slides and verses are rows**, with a 1px separator and no card; the state is the row's background only (on air gold, Prévia green, the next slide `bg-2`/`bg-3`; a note is a mark in the corner). A slide keeps the editor's lines, one line each, ending in "…" when too long; verses wrap. The No ar strip, low across the window, keeps its cards.

A row's hover actions (Favoritar, Editar, Adicionar) never cover the middle of the row, because a click there prepares: in the Biblioteca's song list (`kx-acts-list`) they show their names from 300px of row width up and shrink to glyphs below it.

**Focus.** Controls reached with the keyboard get the 2px `brand` ring, inset so it never grows the control. A text field holding the caret gets no gold ring: a quiet inset 1px `fg-3` line, at least 3:1 against the field (owner 2026-10-16). **Cursor:** buttons keep the Windows arrow (owner 2026-10-16); the hand is for links only.

**The Programa** has no header and no "Programa" word: its state is the pill inside its screen, bottom-right — a dot in the state's colour (`air` red on air, `rh` blue with *Travado* when locked, `warn` amber when an output failed, grey *Nada no ar*), the title in that colour when the player does not already name the item, the position and *2 telas*, then the actions. The monitor has **no frame while it is simply on air**; a frame appears only for a state that needs attention: blue `rh` when locked, amber `warn` on a failure. With the screens locked the Programa still shows the real screen. **The Prévia** has no card: its caption is the badge in its top-left corner (*Prévia · nome · 2/6*, blue *Prévia · travado* while locked). The amber hatch belongs to a Prévia whose file is missing (*Arquivo não encontrado*).

The *Aviso rápido* goes to the screens at once (the field says so); while one is showing it becomes a `kx-alert` line (warn bar, warn tint, elapsed time, *Tirar*); on the screens it is one bar in each output's look (an amber band across the top by default). Tools (Relógio, Cronômetro, Timer, Texto livre) are `kx-tool` ghost buttons that go through the Prévia like any item and render on screen as `kx-screen-tool`. **Mensagens prontas** (`AlertPreset` rows): the star beside *Mostrar* keeps the field's text; the kept messages are chips under the field — a click puts one in the field, a double click shows it at once, the cross deletes it.

Banners are one 28px line across the grid, tinted, with a 2px top rule in the state colour: blue for `TELAS TRAVADAS — nada novo vai às telas` (with *Destravar*), amber for an output that failed (its title in `alert-title`, the detail in grey, the way out as buttons: *Reabrir*, *Ver saídas*, *Esquecer*). Output banners never stack with each other: one at a time, with "e mais N" for the others. The blue lock banner is separate and can sit with one.

An output is in one of these states, always with a word: *Conectada*, *Reconectando…*, *Desconectada às 19:42* (a network screen that left), *Fechada às 19:30* (a window that closed), *Fechada* (never opened this session), *Conectada · travada* (screens locked). The Saídas status in the top bar is compact (*2 telas*, amber with a problem, *Nenhuma saída aberta*, blue while locked), the full sentence in its tooltip; a green dot never sits beside zero.

## Iconography

Bare glyphs from **Lucide** (owner 2026-10-19, Rodada HI), mapped to the names the code uses in `src/lib/icons.js` (`ICON_NODES`; Lucide's `lock-open` is our `unlock`, `ellipsis` our `more`, `library` our `media`…) and drawn by `Icon.jsx`. Three are our own, as nodes in the same file, because Lucide has no twin: `black` (Tela preta), `logo` and `synced`. A new icon is one import from `lucide` and one entry in the map, never an inline SVG. 24-unit grid drawn for 16px, **1.5 stroke** (Lucide's default is 2; `STROKE` in icons.js is the one place to change it, kept one pixel at any size), round caps and joins, no fill, no box, border or background around them (the exceptions: the top bar's Ver and bell sit in round `bg-1` chips, because the bar itself has no panel, and the Programa's actions sit in its pill). A glyph sits at `fg-2` and lightens to `fg-1` on hover; a glyph next to a label that already says the same thing is removed. The way back inside THE window is the *← Voltar* chip in its bar, from the second level; the (x) closes it. Shortcuts appear in tooltips (`Tela preta · B`, `Enviar ao ar · Enter (Ctrl+Enter de qualquer lugar)`), never printed on buttons.

## Motion

Hover, focus, selection and state changes: `duration-fast` (120ms) with `ease-out`, on background-color, color, border-color and opacity; popovers and menus appear in place. The motion the console does have, each with a purpose:
- **Folds** — a panel folding to its header or strip, No ar folding away when no lyrics are on air: **180ms** (owner 2026-10-16).
- **Dragging in a list** — the rows around the slot glide and the card settles into it: 180ms `ease-out`.
- **THE window** — the frame animates between its two sizes.

On the projected screens (and the console's moving monitors, which are those screens), a slide change uses the theme's transition; Esc, Limpar, Tela preta and Logo **fade out** as Configurações › Telas e saídas › Transições says (the screen drawn before goes transparent over the one now), and a sound that another one stops fades out the same way. Outputs ignore reduced motion (the audience sees them); still previews cut. `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` removes every console transition above.
